Concept: a minimal mint T whose stem is interrupted by a diagonal tripwire cut.
This is Tripwire's identity, not a replacement for Nansen attribution or venue/token logos.

Master: `tripwire-source.png` (generated transparent PNG).
Exports: `apps/{extension,web}/public/logos/tripwire.png`, extension `public/icons/icon-{16,32,48,128}.png`, website `app/icon.png`.
Regenerate pixel-size exports with `node scripts/export-tripwire-logo.mjs`.
The site/popup mark preserves alpha. Browser icons use a dark backing for legibility on light toolbars.

## Generation prompt

A distinctive bold geometric abstract capital T made from a wire: a broad horizontal upper bar and a centered strong vertical stem, with one precise diagonal interruption/cut in the stem evoking a tripwire signal. Extremely simple confident silhouette, two or three filled geometric pieces maximum. Flat solid electric mint green #00FFA7, genuinely transparent background with alpha. Square canvas, icon occupies about 80% of canvas with equal margins; centered optically. Crisp clean vector-like edges, consistent geometry, lightly softened corners, works at 16 pixels. No gradients, no glow, no shadow, no texture, no circles or surrounding badge, no shield, no eye, no lightning bolt, no charts, no additional letters or marks. Must be original, not Nansen logo and not a copy of another brand.
