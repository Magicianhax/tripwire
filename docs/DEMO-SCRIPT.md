# Tripwire demo video script

**Target duration:** approximately 2 minutes. Treat the timestamps as editing targets and adjust cuts to the recorded narration.

**Delivery:** natural, conversational TTS or voiceover at roughly 135–145 words per minute—not a trading advertisement.

**Core story:** understand what you’re looking at before you trade. Introduce Nansen early and make the exit-liquidity warning the payoff.

## Scene-by-scene script

### 00:00–00:10 — Hook: “Am I exit liquidity?”

**Scene**

Open directly on the real Uniswap warning. Keep **“Trade blocked by your rules”** and **“I AM EXIT LIQUIDITY”** visible. Slowly zoom toward the warning; leave the override input empty.

**Voiceover**

> Before you click swap, there’s one question worth asking: am I becoming someone else’s exit liquidity? Tripwire puts the evidence closer to that decision.

**On-screen text**

`Before you trade, look closer.`

### 00:10–00:24 — Introduce Tripwire and Nansen

**Scene**

Cut to the website hero. Show the Tripwire logo, **Powered by Nansen**, and the main headline. Then transition into an actual browser tab running the extension.

**Voiceover**

> Tripwire is a browser extension that brings Nansen’s onchain intelligence into the sites you already use. Instead of breaking your workflow to investigate, you can open the context right where you are.

**On-screen text**

`Tripwire · Powered by Nansen`

### 00:24–00:41 — X: a name becomes a profile

**Scene**

Open Vitalik’s public X profile. Briefly highlight the Nansen badge beside the name, then click it.

Show the entity summary, followed by Holdings or Performance. Avoid scrolling rapidly.

**Voiceover**

> Start on X. When a supported profile has a Nansen match, a badge opens its available entity insights. You can inspect reported holdings, portfolio composition, and performance—without leaving the post or profile you were reading.

**On-screen text**

`From a name to onchain context.`

### 00:41–00:59 — DEXes: inspect the exact token

**Scene**

Switch to Jumper with a token selected. Highlight the Tripwire status strip, then open Details.

Cut briefly to the CHUMP example on Dexscreener. Point to the chain and contract in the card header, then the wallet-flow breakdown.

**Voiceover**

> On Jumper and Dexscreener, Tripwire adds context to the selected token. Check its chain and contract, inspect wallet flows, and explore the available buyers, sellers, and holders. Other markets stay separate, so a matching ticker doesn’t become a different token’s verdict.

**On-screen text**

`Exact token. Exact chain.`

### 00:59–01:15 — Hyperliquid: perp-specific insights

**Scene**

Open ETH on Hyperliquid and click Tripwire’s Details control.

Show Positioning first. Then switch to Liquidations and briefly inspect a chart bar. Keep the order form untouched.

**Voiceover**

> On Hyperliquid, the view stays focused on perps. Compare reported long and short positioning, review funding, and inspect liquidation levels for the returned positions. It’s another perspective alongside your chart—not an instruction to take a trade.

**On-screen text**

`Positioning · Funding · Liquidation levels`

### 01:15–01:29 — Polymarket: inspect the trader

**Scene**

Open the public Polymarket leaderboard. Highlight a Nansen badge beside a trader’s name.

Click it and switch to the Polymarket tab. Show the available performance summary and positions.

**Voiceover**

> On Polymarket, a badge beside a trader lets you open available wallet insights. Look beyond the leaderboard number and inspect their prediction-market activity, performance, and positions—all from the same page.

**On-screen text**

`Look beyond the leaderboard.`

### 01:29–01:49 — Payoff: the exit-liquidity warning

**Scene**

Return to the genuine GIZA warning on Uniswap.

Show, in order:

1. The rule that fired.
2. **See who’s selling**.
3. The **I AM EXIT LIQUIDITY** confirmation phrase and disabled Override button.

Do **not** type the phrase or press Override.

**Voiceover**

> And when one of your configured rules fires, Tripwire makes you pause. Here, a drawdown rule has triggered the warning. You can inspect the evidence before going further. Continuing requires an explicit acknowledgement: “I am exit liquidity.” The friction is deliberate. The decision is still yours.

**On-screen text**

`A deliberate pause before proceeding.`

### 01:49–02:00 — Close

**Scene**

Cut back to the clean website hero or a simple end card with the Tripwire logo and prominent Nansen attribution.

**Voiceover**

> Tripwire doesn’t promise that a trade is safe. It helps you make a more informed decision. Nansen intelligence, inside your workflow. Tripwire: look closer before you trade.

**On-screen text**

`Tripwire`  
`Powered by Nansen`

If the repository is available when you publish, add:

`Download link in the description`

Otherwise use:

`Extension release coming soon`

## Recording notes

- **Record the actual extension interactions**, not the website’s screenshot slider. The slider works as a closing shot, but real clicks make the demo convincing.
- **Use logged-out pages and public profiles.** Keep personal balances, connected wallet addresses and account details out of frame.
- **Wait for each card to load before recording the usable take.** Trim waiting time in the edit; don’t replace missing values or alter figures.
- **Keep the relevant card readable.** Record at 1080p or higher, then crop into the badge, card or warning instead of showing a tiny full-browser view throughout.
- **Don’t narrate exact prices, balances or percentages.** They change, and the demo should remain accurate after recording.
- Use a quiet music bed, simple cuts and a slight pause before **“I am exit liquidity.”** That is the moment to let the screen speak.
