built Tripwire, a browser extension that brings @nansen_ai data into the sites where you already research and trade: X, Hyperliquid, Uniswap, Jumper, Jupiter, DEX Screener, pump.fun and Polymarket.

i wanted to stop copying addresses between tabs every time i had a question about a token or wallet.

on X, click a badge beside someone’s name to explore their available Nansen profile, or open a token mention to check its markets.

on DEXes, see who’s buying and selling, wallet flows, holder concentration and trader performance beside the token you’re looking at.

on Hyperliquid, check perp positioning, funding and liquidation levels. on Polymarket, inspect holder positioning for the outcome you’ve selected.

you can also set your own rules. when a trade triggers them, Tripwire shows the evidence and can block the trade button until you acknowledge the warning.

yes, one of the overrides makes you type “I am exit liquidity.”

it won’t tell you a trade is safe. it gives you more to look at before you click.
